TASK 2 - SWITCH OFF TWO PAGE TYPES     TIME: 30 min   CAP: 45 min
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY: WordPress automatically creates a separate web page for every photo
we upload, and for every tag. They are empty pages. Google is finding
them and it makes our whole website look lower quality.

     Yoast SEO > Settings > Advanced > Media pages.
     Find "Enable media pages" and switch it OFF.

     Rank Math > General Settings > Links.
     Find "Redirect Attachments" and switch it ON.
   Click Save.

   CHECK: open https://marketeersresearch.com/media/karim/ in a new tab.
   It should now show the photo file itself, or a "not found" page. It
   should NOT show a normal web page with our header and menu.

PART B - Hide the tag pages (15 minutes)
   - If YOAST SEO:
     Yoast SEO > Settings > Content types > Categories & tags > Tags.
     Set "Show Tags in search results?" to OFF.
   - If RANK MATH:
     Rank Math > Titles & Meta > Tags.
     Set "Robots Meta" to Custom, then tick "No Index".
   Click Save.

                                     TIME: 2.5 hours   CAP: 3.5 hours

WHY: This is the most important task of the month, so take your time
with it. Every blog post shows its date as a clickable link. Those links

STEP 1 - Record the "before" state.

   1. In the WordPress left menu, find and click "The7".
   2. Click "Theme Options".

   4. Look for a sub-section called "Post Meta", "Meta Information" or

      Comments.
   6. Switch DATE to OFF. Leave everything else exactly as it is.
   7. Click "Save Changes" at the top.

   STOP. Screenshot the screen you are on and send it to me. Do not
   change any other setting. Then go to Step 4 anyway and report what
   you find.

   1. WordPress left menu: WP-Optimize > Cache. Click "Purge cache".
   2. Log into Sucuri. Find "Clear cache" and click it.

   1. Open the SAME blog post from Step 1.
   2. Press Ctrl+Shift+R to force a fresh load.
   3. Right-click > View page source > Ctrl+F > type:  /blog/2026/
   4. It should now say 0 matches.
