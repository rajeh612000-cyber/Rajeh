## Before you send

- Replace `{{first_name}}` with your email tool's merge tag (Mailchimp `*|FNAME|*`, HubSpot `{{contact.firstname}}`), with "there" as the fallback.
- Keep the unsubscribe link and your company's postal address in the footer. Most email tools add both for you.
- Subject 1 mentions 2027. If the email goes out after planning season, use subject 2 or 3.
- If the list is big enough, send subject 1 to half of it and subject 2 to the other half, then keep the one with more opens.
