# Build order

Do it in this order so nothing gets thrown away.

## 1. Stills (one evening)

- Generate the character reference. Lock it.
- Generate the desk still for shot 2. Lock it. Shots 6, 7 and 8 inherit from it.
- Generate stills for shots 1, 3, 4, 5, 9A, 9B.
- Cut a nine frame animatic from the stills, timed to the EDL, in Premiere, Resolve or CapCut. Watch it at 24 fps. This is the first time you will feel the rhythm. Fix the rhythm here, not later.

## 2. Sound sketch (one hour)

- Lay in the sub hit, the drone, the gym bass hits, the clack, the tick and the arc tone against the animatic. Rough is fine.
- Watch the animatic with sound. If the gym does not feel like a jolt, the corridor before it is too loud or the gym is too long.

## 3. Video generations (two or three evenings)

- Shots 2, 6, 7, 8, 9A first, because they share the desk and the character and they are the hardest to make match.
- Then 1 and 9B, the aerials.
- Then 3 and 5, the crowds.
- Then 4, the gym, with the most variants.
- For each shot, pick the best 24 to 29 frames and drop them into the animatic in place of the still.

## 4. Comps (one evening)

- Track the monitors in shots 2, 6, 7, 8. Build the screen comps flat, corner pin them on.
- Nameplate slide and the word "Senior".
- Render bar and the snap.
- The map dissolve and the arc.
- The whip pan between 3 and 4.

## 5. Grade (one evening)

- Build the cold node tree on shot 2 until it is right. Copy it to everything.
- Build the warm tree on shot 4.
- Grain, flare and vignette over all of it, last.

## 6. Mix (one evening)

- Replace the sketch sounds with real ones. Keep the structure from the sketch.
- Mix to minus 14 LUFS. Check on a phone speaker.

## 7. Export and loop check

- Export per the delivery spec. Watch it loop five times on a phone. Post.

## What to keep

- Every still, every clip variant, every version of the timeline. Name them by shot and version. In a month you will want to recut this at 15 seconds or as a 6 second ad, and the assets are the expensive part.
- The finished file goes in your portfolio folder the same day. It is proof of a skill you said you wanted. Treat it like a case study, not a post.
