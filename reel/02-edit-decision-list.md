## Shot by shot

### 1. The fall (frames 0 to 28)

What it says: the city is asleep and one person is not.

- Frame 0 already has the lit window in frame, small, warm, dead centre lower third. The camera is already falling. Nothing in this reel starts from rest.
- Haze is thick at the top and thins as we descend. Hundreds of dark windows, a few sodium street lamps far below, no cars.
- The lit window grows from a point to a rectangle that fills the frame by frame 28. Warm light, cool everything else.
- Bass hit on frame 0. Air rush rises through the shot.

### 2. The orbit (frames 29 to 57)

What it says: the room is built for twenty and holds one.

- Wide open plan office at night. Rows of empty desks, chairs pushed in, monitors black. One desk lit by two monitors. Two brand marks glow on the screens, comped in post.
- Him from behind and to the side. Charcoal overshirt, watch on the left wrist on the desk. Never the face.
- 40 degree orbit, slow, heavy. The empty desks pass in the foreground as soft dark shapes.
- Practical sound only. HVAC, one mouse click.

### 3. The glass room (frames 58 to 81)

What it says: the party is on the other side of the glass, and he is in the corridor.

- Corridor lit cold blue from ceiling strips. At the end, a glass meeting room with warm light and six to eight silhouettes mid laugh, gestures, someone leaning back.
- Camera stays in the corridor, never enters. Creeps forward maybe 3 percent of the frame over the shot.
- His reflection on the glass, foreground right, out of focus, a shape with a shoulder and the edge of a jaw. Not a face.
- Laughter is behind the glass, low passed. Fluorescent hum in the corridor is louder than the laughter.

### 4. The gym (frames 82 to 105)

What it says: this is the one place where effort is visible.

- Only warm shot. Only fast shot. Only handheld shot. Only saturated shot.
- A vertical shaft of light through dust. A loaded barbell on the way up, flexing under the plates. Chalk. Sweat on a forearm and the watch. No face.
- Enter on a whip pan from the corridor, built in post. Exit on a hard cut as the bar comes down.
- Sound goes full band. Iron, breath, a single distorted bass hit at frame 82 and a second at frame 96.

### 5. The keeper (frames 106 to 129)

What it says: he did his job, and the celebration is sixty yards away.

- Night pitch, floodlights, low mist. Keeper alone in his box, gloves on knees or standing square. Back to us or profile.
- Sixty yards upfield, a pile of players celebrating, backs turned, blurred to shapes.
- 85 mm compression so the distance reads as a chasm. Slow push from behind the goal.
- Sound drops to almost nothing after the gym. Distant crowd, low passed. Wind. A whistle far away.

### 6. The day (frames 130 to 157)

What it says: sending, sending, sending. Nothing comes back.

- Same desk, same angle family as shot 2 so the viewer knows where they are. Time lapse from dawn through dusk. A sun bar crosses the wall and the floor.
- On the monitor, comped: application cards stacking, each with a small "Sent" state, stacking up the screen. In the corner, an inbox counter, reading 0 the entire shot. It never moves.
- He is in the chair the whole time, a blur of small movements. Watch catches light once.
- Sound: an accelerating tick, a "sent" whoosh each card. No inbox chime, ever.

### 7. The nameplate (frames 158 to 181)

What it says: promoted, in name.

- Frame 158 is macro on a brushed metal nameplate sliding into frame from the left. It stops with a clack at frame 163. It reads "Senior". Comped in post.
- Camera pulls straight back over 18 frames to the shot 2 wide. Same chair, same monitors, same man from behind, same two logos glowing.
- Nothing else has changed. That is the shot.
- Sound: one metal slide, one clack, then room tone.

### 8. The render (frames 182 to 210)

What it says: the work finishes, and no one is there to see it.

- Over the shoulder on one monitor. A render bar at 62 percent, crawling. Fan hum.
- Frames 190 to 198: his hand types one short command. Five keys and enter. The only clean sound in the shot.
- Frame 199: the bar snaps to 100 percent. One high, dry tick. Everything else cuts to silence for three frames.
- Frames 199 to 210: a slow 6 frame push toward the screen, then hold. Nobody in the office. Nobody reacts.

### 9. The arc (frames 211 to 239)

What it says: he is aiming somewhere he has not arrived.

- He stands. The camera retreats, out through the window into the night air. The window becomes the lit rectangle from shot 1. The building becomes the block. The block becomes the city. Ramp speed from 1x to 2.5x through this.
- Around frame 224 the city lights resolve into a dot map. Cairo is a warm cluster. The Mediterranean is a dark band.
- Frame 226 to 234: one thin line arcs north from Cairo, ease out, and stops over open sea about two thirds of the way across. Tiny overshoot back at 234.
- Frame 236: the tip pulses once, faintly.
- Hold to 239. If you want black, three frames at most. Loop.

## Continuity constants

These are what make nine generated clips read as one film.

- The man: charcoal overshirt, dark short hair, watch on the left wrist. Back, side, silhouette, reflection. Never the face.
- The desk: two monitors, left one slightly angled, black chair, a single mug, the nameplate slot empty until shot 7.
- The palette: blue black shadows, teal blue midtones, desaturated highlights. Gym is the exception.
- The grain: one grain layer over everything at the same intensity. This is the single most effective unifier for mixed AI footage.
- The flare: one anamorphic flare pass on every practical light, added in post, same colour temperature.
