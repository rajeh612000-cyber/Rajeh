The Unlanded Arc

Nine shots, two hundred and forty frames, one warm break, no dialogue. A man doing more than anyone can see, in a room nobody else is in, aiming at a place he has not reached yet.

Why the cut is shaped like this

  1. Frame one is the whole reel. Start already inside the fall with the lit window visible at frame 0. The bass hit lands on frame 0. Nothing in this piece starts from rest.
  2. Never show the face. Reflection, back, silhouette. It is what makes nine generated clips read as one man, and it makes the viewer become him. Anchor identity with a charcoal overshirt and a watch on the left wrist.
  3. One warm shot, cut short. The gym is the only heat, speed, saturation and handheld in the reel. If it feels good, it is too long.
  4. Two still beats, two different kinds of still. The nameplate is a cut on action told dry. The render bar is where the sound drops out so the snap to 100 is the cleanest tick in the piece.
  5. Cut for the loop. Frame 239 is the map, frame 0 is Cairo from above. Same grammar, so the loop reads as "and again". Black is three frames at most.
  6. Every screen is a comp. Generate blank glowing screens. Add the logos, the cards, the counter, the bar and the nameplate in post. Generated text is never sharp and never says what you need.

Warm break on the gym only. Read at 3200 K, saturation up a quarter, contrast up, blacks crushed here and nowhere else, halation on every bright edge.

Over everything, last: one fine 35 mm grain layer at one intensity, one anamorphic streak pass on every practical, a subtle top heavy vignette. Grain is the single biggest trick for making mixed generated footage cut together.

Build order

  1. Stills for every shot, then a nine frame animatic. Fix the rhythm here.
  2. Sound sketch against the animatic. Hit, drone, gym, clack, tick, arc tone.
  3. Video generations, desk shots first. Pick the best second of each, not the best clip.
  4. Comps: screens, nameplate, bar, map and arc, whip pan.
  5. Grade, then grain and flare last. Mix. Export. Loop check.
