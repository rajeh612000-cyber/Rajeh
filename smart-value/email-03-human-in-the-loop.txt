Two of Susan's clients ran the same test. They put her analysis next to a report
an AI had written from the same interviews.

Both AI reports missed the insight. It wasn't in what most people said. Susan
heard it in something nobody quite put into words.

That's the part that stayed with me. An AI moderator moves to the next question
the moment someone stops talking. Susan waits, because the pause is often where
the answer is.

  "No one knows everything."
  - Susan Fader

She isn't against AI, and she's specific about where it belongs. Whether a box
should be blue or red is a job for AI. Strategy still needs a person. She also
uses it at the start of a study, to question her own assumptions before any data
comes in.
