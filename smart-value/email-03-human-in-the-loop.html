Last week I wrote about a conversation in Amsterdam, and a point Ravneet made that I haven't stopped thinking about: good data and good tools go nowhere if the people using them don't trust them.

This week's conversation came at the same idea from the other side.

 

Susan Fader

Founder of Fader Focus and an MRS Research Hero. She has used AI in her qualitative work since 2014.

In conversation with Mariam Ghabrial, for our series Human in the Loop.

Two of Susan's clients ran the same test. They put her analysis next to a report an AI had written from the same interviews.

Both AI reports missed the insight. It wasn't in what most people said. Susan heard it in something nobody quite put into words.

That's the part that stayed with me. An AI moderator moves to the next question the moment someone stops talking. Susan waits, because the pause is often where the answer is.

 

No one knows everything.

SUSAN FADER

She isn't against AI, and she's specific about where it belongs. Whether a box should be blue or red is a job for AI. Strategy still needs a person. She also uses it at the start of a study, to question her own assumptions before any data comes in.

We also got into why the person who moderates the interviews should be the one who does the analysis. The full episode is on LinkedIn.
