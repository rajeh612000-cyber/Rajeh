Steaks & Beef Cuts

Beef — primal cuts, premium quality

Sourced from the finest beef and prepared to professional standards. Each cut is selected for its texture, marbling and flavour.

The King

Rib-Eye Steak

ريب أي ستيك

From the longitudinal back muscles. Well-marbled with evenly distributed fat that melts on the grill for a rich, robust flavour.

T-Bone Steak

تي بون ستيك

From the lower back, attached to the vertebrae — a low-mobility region that delivers a distinctive, premium flavour.

Roast Beef

عرق روستو

A masterfully selected cut, slow-cooked to balance tenderness with deep flavour — lean yet succulent.

Sirloin Cap

سوبوكو بقري

From the upper leg. Connective collagen between the muscle fibres melts during cooking, giving a gelatinous, textured bite.

Fillet

فيليه

The leanest, most tender cut — virtually fat-free with a clean flavour that melts in the mouth.

Short Rib

شورت ريبس

Bone-in chuck cut. Rewards long, slow cooking with deeply developed flavour and tender meat.

Brisket

بريسكت

Well-marbled cut from the breast. Low-and-slow cooking breaks down its connective tissue into a juicy, succulent texture.

Showpiece

Tomahawk Steak

توما هوك ستيك

A rib-eye left on the long rib bone — quality, flavour and a striking presentation, in one cut.

Lamb Cuts

Lamb — tender, lean, naturally flavourful

Young spring lamb prepared for grilling, slow cooking and traditional Middle Eastern dishes.

Spring Lamb

خروف ضاني

Young spring lamb — low fat content, delicate flavour and a beautiful pink colour.

Lamb Shoulder

كتف الضأن

From the front portion. Tender meat with a rich, savoury taste — ideal for slow roasts and grills.

Lamb Leg

رجل الضأن

From the hind quarter. Tender and lean — the classic choice for kebabs and grills.

Lamb Shank (Top of Leg)

موزة الضأن

Natural gelatin between the fibres enhances tenderness — exceptional in stews and braises.

Veal Cuts

Veal — refined cuts for considered cooking

Selected veal cuts for quick grills, long braises and bone-in showpieces.

Veal Kebab

كباب بتلو

Tender veal from leg or back, cubed for grilling. Low fat, fast cooking, firm texture.

Veal Shank

موزة بتلو

Bone and marrow with rich connective tissues — slow cooking turns it incredibly tender.

Osso Buco (with bone)

سبوكو بالعظم

Cross-cut leg with marrow at the centre. Deep flavour and luxurious texture from a long, gentle braise.

Veal Ribs

ريش بتلو

Bone-in rib cut with a tender layer of meat and just enough fat to keep it juicy on the grill.
